# Crunchix Raw: one-knob lo-fi bitcrusher

![Crunchix Raw free one-knob lo-fi bitcrusher UI](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/RemiBlaze/CrunchixRaw/main/crunchixraw-ui-screenshot.png)

**One CRUNCH knob for instant bitcrush and lo-fi grit.**

Crunchix Raw is the free, one-knob version of Crunchix. Turn the single CRUNCH knob and your sound gets dirtier and more digital, with Auto Gain keeping the level matched so it just gets crunchier, never louder.

**macOS** (Apple Silicon and Intel): AU, VST3, CLAP, AAX, Standalone. Signed and notarized by Apple.

**Windows** 10 and 11, 64-bit: VST3, CLAP, Standalone. Authenticode signed.

AAX ships on macOS only.

---

## 🚀 Download & Install

Go to the [latest release](https://github.com/RemiBlaze/CrunchixRaw/releases/latest) and pick your platform.

**macOS**
1. Download **`CrunchixRaw_Installer.pkg`**.
2. Double-click it and follow the installer. It is signed and notarized by Apple, so it installs cleanly with no security warnings.
3. Restart your DAW and rescan plug-ins. Crunchix Raw appears under **Remi Blaze**.

**Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit**
1. Download **`CrunchixRaw_Installer.exe`**.
2. Run it and follow the installer. It is Authenticode signed.
3. Restart your DAW and rescan plug-ins. Crunchix Raw appears under **Remi Blaze**.

No dongle and no extra account on either platform.

Full guide: **[remiblaze.com/support](https://remiblaze.com/support/)**.

---

## 🎛️ What It Does
One big **CRUNCH** knob. As you turn it up, Crunchix Raw reduces bit depth (harder digital quantization) and adds sample-rate decimation together for instant lo-fi grit. Auto Gain stays on to keep the output level-matched, so pushing the knob adds dirt without a volume jump. Runs at zero added latency and works on drums, synths, vocals, and full mixes.
