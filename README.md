# Crunchix Raw — one-knob lo-fi bitcrusher

![Crunchix Raw](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/RemiBlaze/CrunchixRaw/main/crunchixraw-ui-screenshot.png)

**One CRUNCH knob for instant bitcrush and lo-fi grit.**

Crunchix Raw is the free, one-knob version of Crunchix. Turn the single CRUNCH knob and your sound gets dirtier and more digital — with Auto Gain keeping the level matched so it just gets crunchier, never louder.

Fully **signed and notarized** for macOS as **AU, VST3, and Standalone**.

---

## 🚀 Download & Install
1. Go to the [latest release](https://github.com/RemiBlaze/CrunchixRaw/releases/latest).
2. Download **`CrunchixRaw_Installer.pkg`**.
3. Double-click it and follow the installer. Signed & notarized by Apple — installs cleanly, no security warnings.
4. Restart your DAW and rescan plug-ins.

Full guide: **[remiblaze.com/support](https://remiblaze.com/support/)**.

---

## 🎛️ What It Does
One big **CRUNCH** knob. As you turn it up, Crunchix Raw reduces bit depth (harder digital quantization) and adds sample-rate decimation together for instant lo-fi grit. Auto Gain stays on to keep the output level-matched, so pushing the knob adds dirt without a volume jump. Runs at zero added latency and works on drums, synths, vocals, and full mixes.
